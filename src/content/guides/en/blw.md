## The short answer

BLW, or baby-led weaning, means your baby gets soft pieces of food in their hand and brings them to their mouth alone. Less spoon-feeding, more exploring. At the beginning milk stays the main meal. The plate is practice and taste, not a finished menu.

In Moonli, BLW is exactly what you need day to day: a **list** that you fill in. Tap ingredients, add your own, see what has already been tried. **Recipes** sit deliberately next to it. They are a separate category for cooking along, not the same feature.

For the wider start to solids, including puree, see [Starting solids](/en/guides/starting-solids/).

## What BLW is, and what it is not

BLW is one way of introducing solids: finger food in a safe shape, your baby eating at their own pace, often at the family table. It is not an obligation, and it is not a contest over who is "puree-free".

It is also not a licence for hard nuts, whole grapes, or anything from the adult plate without a second look. Shape, softness, and supervision matter. Gagging while learning is common; trouble breathing or swelling is an emergency. If you are unsure, ask. A list cannot replace an assessment in person.

The WHO and the NHS describe starting solids around six months, alongside milk. Whether your baby is ready is for you and a professional to decide, not a hashtag.

## When families start with BLW

Signs that often show up together: sitting with support, interest in food, bringing things to the mouth, a weaker tongue thrust. None of them alone is a starting gun. Together they are worth a conversation.

For some families BLW fits because the baby loves to grab. For others the spoon works better, or a mix of both. Moonli does not judge that. The list only remembers what happened.

## The BLW list in Moonli

### What you tick off

More than 100 ingredients are ready: vegetables, fruit, grains, protein and more. If something is missing, you add it yourself. Tap, onto the plate, done. Later you can see what has already been through, including for the person who feeds next time.

The list is a memory, not a verdict. A day with two soft pieces and plenty on the bib is a complete day.

### What the list does not do

It does not cook for you. It does not tell you which recipe is "due". It does not replace advice on allergies or safety. It records which foods have already appeared, so nobody has to ask about the carrot in a chat message.

### Recipes: a separate category

When you cook something along or want to keep how you made it, those are **recipes** in Moonli, separate from BLW. There you write down what went in and how. Optionally you share it with the community. BLW stays the ingredient list; recipes stay the cookbook.

So it stays clear: the list shows what your baby has already explored. A recipe shows how you prepared a dish.

## Safe and calm at the table

- soft, graspable pieces in an age-appropriate shape
- baby upright and supervised
- one new food at a time, if you want to watch for reactions
- keep offering milk: it carries the nutrition while the mouth practises
- trouble breathing, swelling, repeated vomiting: get help straight away, do not note it down first

A face that hates the taste, or a gag on the first piece, is often practice. Uncertainty is a good reason to ask.

## How BLW sits beside everything else

The list lives next to milk feeds, sleep, and growth. You see the day as a sequence, without three apps. If weight is on your mind, the curve is nearby, not as pressure to empty the plate.

Both people who feed see the same status: what has been tried, what is new. That takes small mental-load questions out of the chat.

## A calm first BLW week

Pick one food with your doctor or midwife that is soft and easy to hold. Offer it without needing the plate to end up empty. Tick off in the list what was there. One sentence is enough: it landed, it barely landed, nothing unusual afterwards.

At the end of the week you have a short list instead of a bad conscience. Milk stays the foundation, so carry on with the [breastfeeding and bottle log](/en/guides/track-breastfeeding-bottles/). For the general start without a BLW focus: [Starting solids](/en/guides/starting-solids/). And if the evening gets restless after eating: [baby sleep routine](/en/guides/baby-sleep-routine/).
